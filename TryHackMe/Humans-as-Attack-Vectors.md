# Humans as Attack Vectors

## Room Information
Platform: TryHackMe

Topic: Social Engineering and Human-Focused Attacks

## Key Concepts Learned

### Human Element
- Humans are often the weakest link in cybersecurity.
- Attackers target people to gain access to systems and information.

### Social Engineering
- Manipulates human psychology instead of exploiting technical vulnerabilities.
- Common techniques include urgency, trust, fear, and curiosity.

### Phishing
- Fake emails designed to steal credentials or deliver malware.
- Indicators include suspicious senders, malicious links, and unexpected attachments.

### Malware Delivery
- Attackers may distribute malware through fake software downloads and malicious websites.

### Deepfakes and Impersonation
- Attackers can impersonate trusted individuals.
- Verification procedures are important before taking action.

### Defensive Measures
- Security awareness training
- Anti-phishing solutions
- Antivirus and EDR solutions
- Access management policies

## Practical Scenarios
Completed SOC analyst exercises involving:
- Phishing detection
- Suspicious login investigations
- Account protection decisions
- Security policy selection

## Takeaways
People are a primary target for cyberattacks. Strong security awareness and proper verification procedures help reduce organizational risk.
